Your Environment

Create Your Learning Space

The quality of your learning depends on the environment you create. Kautilya himself said — a focused mind absorbs wisdom, a distracted one loses it.

Physical Space Preparation
  • Choose a quiet, dedicated room Must Find a room where you will not be interrupted. Inform family members / roommates that you are in a live class from 6:00–8:00 PM and you should not be disturbed.
  • Lock the door or put a "Do Not Disturb" sign Must A single unexpected interruption can break your concentration during a key insight. A small sign on the door works wonders.
  • Turn off the TV and surrounding noise Must Background TV or music interferes with focused learning. Silence all entertainment devices in your room and nearby rooms if possible.
  • Comfortable seating with good posture Should Sit on a proper chair at a table, not on a bed or sofa. Good posture keeps your mind alert and prevents drowsiness during the 90-minute session.
  • Good lighting in the room Should Avoid attending in a dark room. Proper lighting keeps you alert and reduces eye strain. Natural light is best; a bright desk lamp works well at 6:00 PM.
  • Keep a glass of water nearby Nice Hydration helps focus. Have water ready so you don't have to leave during the session.
Phone & Digital Distractions
  • Put your phone on Silent / DND mode Must Enable "Do Not Disturb" on your phone. Calls, WhatsApp messages, Instagram notifications — everything can wait 2 hours. Your growth cannot.
  • Place your phone face-down or in another room Should Even a face-up phone with a lit notification takes 20+ minutes of focus to recover from, according to research. Out of sight = out of mind.
  • Close all unnecessary browser tabs on your laptop Must Before joining, close YouTube, social media, email, news — everything. Keep only the Zoom window open. This removes click-temptation entirely.
  • Turn off desktop / laptop notifications Should On Windows: Focus Assist → Priority Only. On Mac: Do Not Disturb. This prevents email pop-ups and system alerts during the session.
  • Inform your contacts you're unavailable 6:00–8 PM on session days Should Send a quick WhatsApp status or message to close contacts on session days. Most people are very understanding when told upfront.
Note-Taking & Learning Setup
  • Keep a dedicated notebook + pen ready Must Handwritten notes improve retention by 34% compared to typing. Use a fresh notebook exclusively for this 4-day program. Label it "Arthashastra – 2026."
  • Prepare 2–3 questions before each session Should Think about a challenge in your current life — business, leadership, relationships. Come with specific questions. CA Avinash's Q&A is where the deepest insights emerge.
  • Join the Zoom call 5 minutes early Must Join by 5:55 PM to test audio, settle in, and be mentally ready when CA Avinash starts. Latecomers often miss the most important framing of the session.
  • Set a calendar reminder for all 4 session dates Must Add reminders for April 4, 11, 18 & 25 at 5:45 PM IST. Set a 15-minute advance reminder so you're never caught off-guard.
  • Keep your microphone muted by default Should Join with your microphone muted to avoid sending background noise to all participants. Unmute only when you wish to ask a question.
  • Have dinner before 7:00 PM on session days Nice A heavy digestion process competes with mental focus. Eat light and early so your mind is sharp and alert for the 6:00 PM session.

Mindset

Arthashastra is not casual reading — it is deep strategic wisdom. Here's how to open your mind to receive it fully.
